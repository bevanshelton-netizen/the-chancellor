## Search-discovery policy

Only stable production aliases should be indexed. Hashed preview/deployment URLs, internal operations tools and any route carrying a `noindex` directive must not be promoted as public search destinations.

RevenueForge AI is intentionally omitted until its stable production alias is confirmed indexable. IZAKHONO Reviews is intentionally omitted until its product identity/routing is corrected.
