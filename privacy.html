Privacy Notice

Your information should be handled with purpose and care.

Effective: 12 August 2026 · The Chancellor’s Business Growth Desk · Powered by Izakhono Africa

This notice explains, in plain language, how information submitted through The Chancellor platform is used. It is an operational privacy notice and should be reviewed by South African legal/privacy counsel before large-scale processing of sensitive personal information.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the service you use, we may collect contact details, business information, audit answers, case descriptions, professional credentials, documents you upload, payment status, communications, appointments, outcomes, ratings and institutional or referral attribution. We do not ask you to provide passwords, banking PINs or card details in case notes or chat.

2. Why we use information

We use information to provide the service you request, assess business readiness or case urgency, create private workspaces, process and verify payments, route eligible cases, review professional credentials, support communication, track service progress, produce approved deliverables, measure confirmed outcomes, maintain security and meet legitimate operational or legal requirements.

3. Financial Rescue and regulated work

The Chancellor may use technology to organise facts and identify urgency. Where the matter requires regulated legal, tax, debt-counselling, financial-advice or other professional work, the platform is designed to route the matter to an appropriately qualified or registered professional. Information is shared only to the extent reasonably required for that service and subject to the applicable access controls.

4. Payments

Online payments are processed through configured payment providers such as PayFast. The Chancellor platform records payment references and transaction status needed to operate the service, but is not intended to store card numbers, CVVs or online-banking credentials.

5. Service providers

We may use hosting, email, messaging, AI, payment and document-processing service providers to operate the platform. These providers process information according to their own service terms and the configuration used by The Chancellor. Optional external channels remain disabled when credentials have not been configured.

6. Public proof

Private case details are not automatically published. Aggregate impact figures use client-confirmed outcomes. Success stories are anonymised and require administrator approval; written testimonials and professional public profiles require separate publication approval.

7. Security and access

The platform uses role-based sessions and private workspaces. Clients, professionals, referral partners and institutional users are intended to access only information authorised for their role. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid uploading information that is not required for the service.

8. Retention

Records may be retained for service delivery, audit, dispute, compliance, financial and operational purposes. Formal retention and deletion periods should be aligned with applicable South African law and the nature of each record before high-volume launch.

9. Your choices

You may ask about information held in relation to your Chancellor service, request correction of inaccurate information, or raise a privacy concern through the contact channel used for your service. Some records may need to be retained where required for lawful operational, financial, dispute or compliance purposes.

10. South African privacy framework

The platform is intended to operate consistently with applicable South African privacy requirements, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). This notice does not itself replace a full legal compliance programme, information-officer process, PAIA documentation, retention schedule or security policy where those are required.

11. Updates

We may update this notice as the platform, service providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current published version.
