# THE CHANCELLOR — Character System

Status: APPROVED DIRECTION / PRODUCTION LOCK

## Brand separation

- THE CHANCELLOR is the character, adviser persona and Business Growth Desk brand.
- BEVAN SHELTON is the premium fashion brand The Chancellor wears.
- Never replace The Chancellor branding with the Bevan Shelton horse crest.
- The horse crest may appear on clothing, lapel pins, accessories, luggage and subtle wardrobe details only.

## Character identity lock

Every visual, avatar, advert, video, animation and website portrait must preserve the same recognisable Chancellor:

- mature Black man, approximately 60 years old;
- completely bald head;
- full, longer white/grey beard;
- narrow gold or rimless glasses;
- broad, mature facial proportions;
- calm intelligent eyes;
- dignified, warm, authoritative presence;
- able to smile, joke, speak, gesture and move naturally without changing identity.

A generic businessman, stock executive, younger version, different beard, different hairline or materially different facial structure is NOT APPROVED.

## Personality on camera

The Chancellor is not a static portrait. He is a lifelike digital personality who can talk naturally, explain business concepts, react, smile, pause, gesture and move with confidence. Tone: wise, cool, direct, warm, solution-focused and premium without being stiff.

## Wardrobe system — Bevan Shelton

Use varied premium Bevan Shelton looks so the character feels alive rather than wearing one suit forever:

1. Executive — tailored black suit, subtle gold details, premium tie, pocket square.
2. Smart casual — black blazer, knit or polo, tailored trousers, premium sneakers.
3. Luxury street — Bevan Shelton bomber, clean tee, premium trousers, sneakers.
4. Travel — coat or jacket, branded luggage/briefcase, understated accessories.
5. Stage — statement jacket with restrained gold detailing, microphone-ready styling.
6. Weekend mentor — premium golfer, relaxed jacket or tracksuit, luxury casual footwear.

## Approved scene families

Use the same character across multiple aspirational settings:

- Chancellor study / library;
- Johannesburg rooftop or skyline;
- premium lounge or restaurant;
- airport / executive travel;
- university or campus;
- factory / manufacturing floor;
- business district / modern office campus;
- stage / conference / keynote;
- studio / podcast / camera address;
- client consultation setting.

## Talking-avatar requirements

For video and interactive use:

- preserve facial identity frame-to-frame;
- natural lip sync and eye movement;
- restrained head movement;
- expressive hands when appropriate;
- warm South African executive presence;
- no caricature or exaggerated animation;
- camera framing must remain premium and believable.

## Core commercial role

The Chancellor is the visible front office for the Business Growth Desk. He must be able to introduce services, diagnose business problems, guide users into the correct pathway and sell the R500 Business Readiness & Growth Audit without making guarantees of funding, contracts or outcomes.

## Production rule

When an approved Chancellor master image is unavailable, use Chancellor crest-led artwork or typography. Never invent a substitute face.
