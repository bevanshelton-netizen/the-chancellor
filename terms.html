Clear support. Clear commercial terms.

These terms govern services purchased through The Chancellor platform. Nothing in these terms removes or limits any right that a customer has under the Consumer Protection Act, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, or other applicable South African law.

1. Platform role

The Chancellor combines AI-assisted business guidance, structured intake, document workflows, Financial Rescue triage, professional routing, case management, payments, institutional access and related support. The platform does not itself replace a registered legal practitioner, tax practitioner, debt counsellor, authorised financial services provider, business rescue practitioner or other regulated professional where the law requires one.

2. No guaranteed outcome

No content, score, recommendation, referral, business plan, proposal, audit, case assessment or professional introduction guarantees funding, investment, tenders, contracts, tax relief, refunds, debt reduction, asset protection, legal success or any other commercial or legal outcome.

3. AI-generated guidance

Some guidance and voice interactions may be generated with AI. AI output may be incomplete or imperfect and should not be treated as professional advice where regulated expertise is required. Paid Growth Desk recommendations and deliverables may include human review as stated in the applicable service.

4. Financial Rescue

Financial Rescue triage is designed to identify urgency and organise facts. It is not legal advice, tax representation, debt counselling, financial advice or a statutory business rescue engagement. Where regulated work is required, the client should engage the appropriately authorised professional.

5. Professional Network

“Chancellor Verified” means that specified credentials were checked by the platform against available evidence at the time of verification. It does not replace statutory registration, licensing, practising rights, professional-body membership, or the client’s own decision about whether to appoint a professional.

6. Client responsibility

Users must provide information that is accurate to the best of their knowledge, disclose material facts relevant to the service, meet external deadlines, review documents before submission and obtain independent professional advice where needed. Users must not ask the platform or a professional to fabricate expenses, hide assets or income, falsify documents, misrepresent facts or evade lawful obligations.

7. Payments and fees

Fees shown on the platform apply to the service selected. Payment confirmation is based on validated provider notifications rather than a browser return page. The R500 Business Readiness Audit and Growth Desk implementation programmes are business services. Professional case fees, deposits, platform commissions and payouts are handled according to the applicable quote, invoice and recorded payment status.

8. Service delivery

The Chancellor’s Business Growth Desk primarily supplies digital and professional services rather than physical goods. After a payment is successfully validated, digital deliverables such as the Business Growth Audit Report, scorecards, implementation proposals and portal access are made available electronically through the client portal or an agreed electronic channel. Where a service includes human advisory, implementation or specialist work, delivery timing and milestones are stated in the applicable proposal, quote or engagement. No physical shipping or courier delivery applies unless a separate purchase specifically states otherwise.

9. Cancellation policy

A customer may request cancellation before a paid service or agreed implementation work has begun. Where work has already started at the customer’s request, any cancellation and amount payable or refundable will take account of work already reasonably performed, commitments already incurred and any rights that apply by law. For immediately delivered digital reports or other digital content, cancellation after the deliverable has been generated or made available is not automatically entitled to a change-of-mind refund, subject always to applicable statutory rights.

10. Refund policy

Refund requests are assessed fairly and in accordance with applicable South African law. A refund may be approved for a duplicate payment, an incorrect payment amount, a service that The Chancellor cannot supply, an approved cancellation before material work begins, or another circumstance where a refund is legally required or commercially appropriate. A refund is not guaranteed merely because a business outcome, funding application, tender, contract, growth target or other desired result was not achieved. Approved refunds will normally be returned through the original payment method where reasonably possible, and payment-provider or banking processing times may apply.

11. How to request a cancellation or refund

Contact The Chancellor’s Business Growth Desk through the contact method shown on the platform and provide the customer name, service purchased, payment reference, reason for the request and any relevant supporting information. Customers must never send card numbers, PINs, passwords, PayFast merchant credentials or other security secrets.

Published impact figures are intended to use client-confirmed outcomes. Success stories, testimonials and professional profiles require separate publication controls. Historical results are not guarantees of future results.

15. Availability

The platform may depend on hosting, payment, AI, email, SMS, WhatsApp and other service providers. Features may be unavailable during outages, maintenance, credential expiry or provider interruptions. Users should not rely on the platform as the sole method for meeting urgent court, tax, regulatory or contractual deadlines.
