Terms, Refunds, Cancellation & Service Delivery

Clear commercial terms for professional business services.

Effective: 25 August 2026 · The Chancellor’s Business Growth Desk · Powered by Izakhono Africa

These terms govern use of The Chancellor platform and purchases made through it. They apply subject to applicable South African law and any written service proposal, quotation or agreement accepted by the client.

1. Platform role

The Chancellor provides business readiness assessments, business advisory support, growth planning, implementation support, document workflows, Financial Rescue triage, professional routing, case management, payments, institutional access and related professional business services. The platform does not replace a registered legal practitioner, tax practitioner, debt counsellor, authorised financial services provider, business rescue practitioner or other regulated professional where the law requires one.

2. Services sold

The services sold through this website are primarily digital and professional services rather than physical goods. These may include the R500 Business Readiness & Growth Audit, digital reports and proposals, advisory sessions, implementation programmes and separately quoted professional support. The exact scope, price and deliverables for larger engagements are shown in the relevant offer, proposal or quotation.

3. No guaranteed outcome

No content, score, recommendation, referral, business plan, proposal, audit, case assessment or professional introduction guarantees funding, investment, tenders, contracts, tax relief, refunds, debt reduction, asset protection, legal success or any other commercial or legal outcome.

4. AI-generated guidance

Some guidance and voice interactions may be generated with AI. AI output may be incomplete or imperfect and should not be treated as professional advice where regulated expertise is required. Paid Growth Desk recommendations and deliverables may include human review as stated in the applicable service.

5. Client responsibility

Users must provide information that is accurate to the best of their knowledge, disclose material facts relevant to the service, meet external deadlines, review documents before submission and obtain independent professional advice where needed. Users must not ask the platform or a professional to fabricate expenses, hide assets or income, falsify documents, misrepresent facts or evade lawful obligations.

6. Payments and fees

Fees shown on the platform apply to the service selected. Payment confirmation is based on validated payment-provider notifications rather than a browser return page. A payment is not treated as complete until the platform receives and validates confirmation from the payment provider. Professional case fees, deposits, platform commissions and payouts are handled according to the applicable quote, invoice and recorded payment status.

7. Service delivery policy

The Chancellor delivers services electronically through the website, client portal, downloadable reports, digital documents, email, virtual consultation and other agreed business-support channels. No physical shipping is required for the standard Business Growth Desk services.

For the R500 Business Readiness & Growth Audit, the client completes the online assessment and, once payment is validated and the required information is complete, the digital score, diagnosis and available report outputs are unlocked or processing begins. Where a human review is required, we aim to complete that review within two business days after receiving the required information and validated payment.

For implementation programmes and bespoke professional work, delivery milestones and estimated completion dates are set out in the accepted proposal, quotation or service plan. Delivery timeframes may move if the client has not supplied required documents, approvals or information, or where a regulated third-party professional is required.

8. Cancellation policy

A client may request cancellation before substantive work has started. Where work has already commenced, documents have been prepared, third-party costs have been committed or professional time has been reserved, a reasonable amount may be retained for work actually performed or costs already incurred, subject to applicable South African law.

For larger implementation programmes, any additional cancellation terms stated in the accepted proposal or quotation also apply. Cancellation requests should be made through the client portal or the contact details published on the website so that the request can be recorded and reviewed.

9. Refund policy

Refund requests are considered fairly and in accordance with applicable South African consumer law. A refund will ordinarily be considered where a duplicate or incorrect charge occurred, where The Chancellor cannot deliver the paid service, or where a refund is otherwise required by law.

Once a customised digital service has been substantially or fully supplied — for example, an audit has been completed and its report or diagnosis has been made available — a change-of-mind refund may not be available to the extent permitted by law. Where only part of a larger engagement has been delivered, any approved refund may be limited to the undelivered portion after taking into account work already completed and non-recoverable third-party costs.

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method where technically possible. Payment-provider and banking processing times apply after the refund has been approved and submitted.

10. Financial Rescue

Financial Rescue triage is designed to identify urgency and organise facts. It is not legal advice, tax representation, debt counselling, financial advice or a statutory business rescue engagement. Where regulated work is required, the client should engage the appropriately authorised professional.

11. Professional Network

“Chancellor Verified” means that specified credentials were checked by the platform against available evidence at the time of verification. It does not replace statutory registration, licensing, practising rights, professional-body membership, or the client’s own decision about whether to appoint a professional.

12. Referrals and commissions

Referral attribution may be recorded. Referral commissions are not automatically payable and may remain disabled where professional, regulatory, contractual or legal restrictions apply.

13. Institutional accounts

Employer, bank, insurer, union, association and other institutional packages are subject to the applicable commercial agreement, SLA, benefit rules, eligibility conditions and service limits. A benefit code does not by itself create an entitlement beyond the agreed institutional arrangement.

14. Public results

Published impact figures are intended to use client-confirmed outcomes. Success stories, testimonials and professional profiles require separate publication controls. Historical results are not guarantees of future results.

15. Availability

The platform may depend on hosting, payment, AI, email, SMS, WhatsApp and other service providers. Features may be unavailable during outages, maintenance, credential expiry or provider interruptions. Users should not rely on the platform as the sole method for meeting urgent court, tax, regulatory or contractual deadlines.

16. Acceptable use

Users may not misuse the service, attempt unauthorised access, upload malicious files, impersonate others, interfere with platform security, or use the service for unlawful, fraudulent or abusive purposes.

17. Privacy

Use of personal information is described in the Privacy Notice. Users should provide only information reasonably required for the service.

18. Disputes and support

If a client has a payment, delivery, cancellation or refund concern, the client should contact The Chancellor through the client portal or the published website contact channels. We will review the transaction, service status and supporting records and respond as soon as reasonably possible.

19. Updates

These terms may be updated as the platform, services or legal requirements evolve. The effective date above identifies the current published version.
