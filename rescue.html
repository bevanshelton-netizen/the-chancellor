The compliance wall

Technology does the triage. Professionals do regulated professional work.

“Chancellor Verified” means specified credentials were checked by the platform against available evidence at the verification date. It does not replace statutory registration, licensing, practising rights or professional-body membership. The Chancellor does not manufacture remedies, promise refunds, erase lawful debt or guarantee case outcomes.

Urgent deadline? Do not rely on this platform as your only method for meeting a court, SARS, creditor, contractual or regulatory deadline. Seek appropriately qualified professional help where required.
